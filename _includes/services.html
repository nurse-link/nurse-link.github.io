Key Features

Request via Icons

Quick and simple requests from non-verbal patients. Patients can express their needs by tapping intuitive icons (e.g., pain, water) on a bedside kiosk. A single touch is enough to send a clear request—no typing required.

Real-Time Nurse Response

Instant notifications and quick responses via nurse app. Nurses receive real-time alerts in both icon and text formats through a mobile app, allowing them to understand the request at a glance and respond without delay.

History & Prioritization

View request history and manage priorities effectively. By checking request history, nurses can detect recurring issues early. When multiple requests are received, the app helps them prioritize efficiently and respond accordingly.
