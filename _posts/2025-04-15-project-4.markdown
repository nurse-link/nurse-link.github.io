description: Based on the selected experience prototype from the Ideation stage, we created a low-fidelity prototype using simple materials such as paper and sticky notes. The prototype simulated key scenarios in which nurses communicate with speech-impaired patients. We conducted user testing with three ENT nurses, observing their interactions and gathering feedback. This process helped us identify usability issues and areas for improvement before moving to a more refined prototype.
